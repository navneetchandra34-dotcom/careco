# CAR Website — Launch checklist

Use this checklist with CAR's leadership before public launch.

## Organization and content
- [ ] Approve the About, Vision, Mission, values, and programme descriptions.
- [ ] Confirm all role holders and names are current.
- [ ] Confirm partner names, relationship descriptions, and permission to use any partner logos (none are used in this initial version).
- [ ] Confirm the reporting periods and supporting records for every numerical highlight.
- [ ] Approve how SDGs are described; resolve the duplicate Goal 8 in the source list.
- [ ] Review claims about registration, tax exemption, NGO Darpan, and CSR eligibility against current official certificates/records.
- [ ] Confirm the approved spelling of names and place names from source reports.

## Safeguarding, privacy, and transparency
- [ ] Confirm written consent or other valid authorization for each recognizable person in the photographs.
- [ ] Apply extra safeguarding checks to photographs showing children and members of vulnerable or tribal communities.
- [ ] Confirm the contact numbers and email address remain active.
- [ ] Decide on a reviewed donor due-diligence pack: current registrations, governance, policies, annual reports, and audited statements as appropriate.
- [ ] Do not add UPI/bank details, a donation checkout, donor tax benefits, or foreign-funding assurances until CAR confirms the accurate and compliant process.
- [ ] If connecting an online form, publish a clear privacy notice and disclose the service receiving submissions.

## GitHub Pages and quality assurance
- [ ] Upload the contents (not the ZIP file) so `index.html` sits at the repository root.
- [ ] Select `main` and `/(root)` under Settings → Pages.
- [ ] Open all seven content pages and the 404 page on desktop and mobile.
- [ ] Test the mobile menu, phone links, email links, and contact email-compose helper.
- [ ] Check image rendering and text contrast at narrow screen widths.
- [ ] Confirm the published URL shown by GitHub Pages. Configure a custom domain only if needed.
