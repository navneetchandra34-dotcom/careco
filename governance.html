The details below come from CAR’s supplied profile and should be checked against original certificates before publication or reliance.

Trust registration

Centre for Action Research is registered as a public trust under the Indian Trust Act, 1882. The organisation profile records registration with the Sub-Registrar, Khandagiri, on 7 December 2016; Document No. 41131609118; Book No. 4; Volume 183.

Income-tax registration under Section 12AB

CAR’s earlier organisational profile refers to registration under Section 12AA. The uploaded Form 10AD order, dated 28 January 2026, records registration under Section 12AB(1)(b), registration number AACTC0432C25HY01, for assessment years 2027–28 to 2036–37. The downloadable order on this page contains the terms issued by the Income Tax Department.

Income-tax approval under Section 80G(5)

Approval number: AACTC0432C26HY01. The Form 10AD order dated 2 September 2026 grants approval under clause (ii) of the second proviso to Section 80G(5) for assessment years 2027–28 to 2031–32. The order states that continued validity depends on a valid Section 12A/12AB registration for the relevant period. Read the downloadable order for the full conditions.
