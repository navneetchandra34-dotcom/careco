Why CAR exists

Healthy ecosystems.
Dignified lives.

For communities connected to rivers, forests and land, environmental protection is also a question of livelihoods, health, identity and rights.

CAR works at that intersection. We listen to community knowledge, create spaces for collective action, strengthen access to information and public systems, and connect local concerns with research, advocacy and institutional collaboration.

PLACE-BASED ACTIONListening before solutions.
From local concern to collective action

Rivers are more than water.
They are life systems.

CAR has supported community action around the Baitarani and Salandi rivers, raising concerns about riverbed encroachment, sand mining, pollution and the consequences of altered flows for farmers and local communities.

Through meetings, field visits, women’s participation and public advocacy, CAR helps make local experience visible in wider conversations about water, ecology and fair access to resources.
