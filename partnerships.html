A partnership approach

Not just funding.
Long-term collaboration.

Strong partnerships begin with a shared understanding of the problem—and a clear agreement about what will be done, how progress will be monitored and how learning will be shared.

CAR’s stated future priorities include scaling small-river restoration pilots, strengthening Forest Rights Act facilitation, building collaborations with CSR and academic institutions, creating a digital knowledge repository and developing community-oriented water monitoring tools.

The opportunities below are suggested pathways for discussion, not pre-approved funding packages. Scope, budget, geography, duration and measurable outcomes should be agreed jointly and supported by a documented project plan.

Partnership design

Clarity before commitment.
Accountability throughout.

Every serious partnership deserves a clear problem statement, an honest budget, realistic outcomes and a plan to learn from implementation.

01

Listen and define
Identify the community priority, context, geography and intended change.

02

Design together
Agree on roles, outputs, timeline, budget, safeguarding and participation.

03

Track progress
Set practical indicators, reporting intervals and responsible data practices.

04

Review and learn
Share findings, recognise limitations and adapt the approach where needed.

Due diligence

Trust is built
with evidence.

Before entering into a funding or CSR arrangement, partners should review current documents, verify eligibility and agree how funds and results will be reported.

Documents to request and verify

✓

Trust registration deed and current registration details.

✓

Current tax registration or approval orders and any 80G documentation being relied upon.

✓

Valid CSR-1 acknowledgement or registration, where relevant to the proposed engagement.

✓

Latest audited financial statements, annual reports and utilisation documentation, as applicable.

✓

Governance policies, safeguarding arrangements and project-specific monitoring plans.

This is a due-diligence checklist; this website does not certify current validity or confirm eligibility for a specific tax or CSR benefit.
