Who we are

Rooted in community.
Committed to justice.

Centre for Action Research (CAR) is a non-profit trust initiated by socially committed activists in 2016 to protect, promote and enrich natural resources while supporting the dignity and rights of the communities who depend on them.

Our story

Protecting resources.
Expanding possibility.

CAR understands that the protection of water, forests, commons, minerals, land and the wider ecology cannot be separated from the well-being and rights of people.

The organisation was initiated in 2016 by like-minded social activists and has its registered office in Bhubaneswar, Odisha. Its work combines public awareness, education, community mobilisation, capacity-building and training with efforts to connect vulnerable communities to resources, institutions and opportunities.

CAR’s approach is grounded in participation: community members are not simply recipients of support. Their lived experience, knowledge and practices are central to defining priorities and sustaining change.

Our purpose

A vision of thriving
communities and ecosystems.

CAR’s stated vision connects ecological conservation with dignity, equality, justice and sustainable livelihoods.

01 / VISION

A world where nature and people thrive together.

Promote collective action to conserve nature, reduce environmental crises and sustain the conditions for all living beings to live in harmony with nature—while enabling communities to live with dignity, equality, justice and sustainable livelihoods.

02 / MISSION

Community participation that becomes practical action.

Promote equal participation in the protection and sustainable development of natural resources while supporting education, health, training, livelihood development and campaigns. Build awareness and networks to address climate change, pollution, poverty and injustice.

How we work

Principles that shape
every intervention.

These principles are drawn from CAR’s supplied organisational profile. They guide how priorities are identified, resources directed and community participation supported.

01

Participatory approach

Prioritise community knowledge and practices, recognising people’s ability to guide and sustain development processes.

02

Equity in resource allocation

Direct attention and resources toward the poorest and most excluded, helping create opportunities to strengthen economic security.

03

Gender equality

Promote women’s participation and leadership in social change, community decision-making and access to opportunities.

Where we work

Local roots.
Wider connections.

CAR is headquartered in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, and its recent activity highlights describe work and engagement across districts in Odisha and beyond.

Its focus includes tribal and indigenous communities, Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups (PVTGs), women and children, and other communities facing social and economic exclusion.
