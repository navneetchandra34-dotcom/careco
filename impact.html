WATER · ECOLOGY · LIVELIHOODS
Case story 01

Keeping rivers in
public conversation.

World Water Day was observed on 22 March 2023 at Sanskruti Bhawan, Bhadrak, in collaboration with Salandi Bikash Manch and Baitarani Bachao Andolan. A field visit with community members and women’s groups drew attention to riverbed encroachment, large-scale sand mining and pollution. On 24 May 2025, a public meeting on Baitarani river issues was held at Kandra near Kanupur Dam; on 12 June 2025, Save Baitarani campaign members met at Bhadrak Town Hall to discuss the Baitarani and Salandi rivers. CAR’s river work also highlights the Nagavali and Vamsadhara rivers, ecological flows, fair access to water and community participation in river protection.

LITERACY · CONFIDENCE · PARTICIPATION
Case story 02

Adult literacy built
around everyday life.

CAR launched an Adult Literacy Programme with Rotary Literacy Mission in July 2024. The supplied annual report describes activity in 12 villages across Rayagada district, supporting adult women and men with little or no formal education. Volunteer teachers used local-language learning materials at community learning centres to strengthen reading, writing and arithmetic for everyday life.

PVTG · DIGNITY · ESSENTIAL SUPPORT
Case story 03

Support shaped by
community consultation.

CAR reports support to more than 800 families from Dongaria and Kutia Kondha communities in Rayagada and Kalahandi. Based on community consultations, assistance included dry rations, hygiene kits and locally preferred essentials such as salt, jaggery, flattened rice and soap.

FOREST REGENERATION · INDIGENOUS SPECIES
Case story 04

Restoration starts
with local knowledge.

For World Environment Day, community members prepared thousands of seed balls using indigenous tree varieties including sal, mahua, mango and tamarind, then dispersed them before the monsoon. This approach draws on local knowledge of forest regeneration and seeks to support natural recovery in forest environments.

FOREST RIGHTS ACT · TRIBAL IDENTITY
Case story 05

Tribal identity and forest rights.

The supplied annual report records a national convention on the Forest Rights Act, 2006, and tribal identity and rights in India, held in Bhubaneswar from 27 February to 1 March 2026. It was organised jointly by AJAM and ActionAid, with Dr. Bratindi Jena of CAR participating as a resource person. The report notes that Union Minister for Tribal Affairs Jual Oram joined online and discussed issues faced by tribal communities.
